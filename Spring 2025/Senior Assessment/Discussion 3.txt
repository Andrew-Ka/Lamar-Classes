Discussion Board of Lesson 3

1. What is the general expectation of a manager from his/her employers?

	* An employer expects his employees to have natural professional skills. One of these key skills is communication in that of both speech and text. 
	* When discussing text-based communication, this majorly includes documents, from first impressions by resume's and cover letters, to professional company documents such as contract documents and reports, to general company communication through memos, business letters, and emails.
	* Employees expect their subordinates to have minimal or virtually no errors in these documents, whether it be in grammar, spelling, or sentence structure. 


2. What are the key factors when writing proposals?

	* Proposals are meant to answer a few questions in response to a proposed solution. A good proposal will answer all of these questions, including the likes of:
		* Will the solution work?
		* Are the costs justified and/or competitive?
		* Are the people of the team experienced?
		* What are alternatives/competitions, and why is this solution the best?
	* Answering all these questions can include these key factors:
		* Time - you don't want to miss a deadline
		* An established theme - a continuous theme helps convince the reader
		* itemize and document cost - this will give a clear picture of what is necessary
		* state your assumptions - this will clarify any doubts



3. How to write a convincing resume cover letter?

	* Cover letters are one of the first documents an employer has about you, making it very valuable. This letter summarizes who you are, including your educational background, skills, and how these match the employer’s needs.
	* Convincing cover letters are well researched, delving into the company and employer. These details sell the fact you want to work for that specific company. Alongside this, by catering your presented experience, you can make it seem like you are a valuable asset and much needed member of the team.
