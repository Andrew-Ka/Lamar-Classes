Discussion Board of Lesson 7

1. Why it is said that a well written document needs many iterations of corrections?

* A document must go through many iterations to refine the document. In woodworking, the project does not end when the wood pieces are put together. They must be sanded down and have many layers of coating applied, whether or not it be a specific paint or varnish. In the same way, writing a first draft helps get the ideas on the page. This, however, is just the material, and the actual final document must be carved out of the first draft. New ideas must be placed in, and unnecessary portions must be taken out, all to refine and create the best version of the document.


2. Which of the ten editing suggestions listed in Lecture 7 is the most important? Why?

* Of the 10 listed suggestions, I believe using a thesaurus is the best way to find the right words for the tasks. Only through my last years of college have I fully understood the necessity of purposeful writing, even finding the specific words to portray the intended meaning. Through usage of a thesaurus, this process has been much easier and has grown my vernacular quite immensely (though I still have a long ways to go). My best recommendation currently are using Thesaurus.com (a classic), or a new website I am currently using, OneLook, which provides more context to each word or phrase.


3. Why it is important to avoid plagiarism?

* Plagiarism is essentially copying information or materials without the proper credit given. Plagiarism is bad morally, but also has consequences far beyond the self. This includes violating academic honest, which can result in expulsion on extreme cases. This could also lead to rejection of submitted professional documents or just mistrust from readers.
