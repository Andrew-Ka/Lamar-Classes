Discussion Board of Lesson 5

1. How important is to do rehearse of a presentation? Give reasons why.

* Speaking is important for a variety of reasons. When preparing a presentation, the oration of a sentence is far different than what is written. Practicing your presentation helps you catch errors, make points quicker, and review the material more. 
* Practicing your presentation orally helps catch error because you are no longer reading or presenting in your head. This allows you to get an outside perspective and catch many types of errors, from simple run-on or incomplete sentences all the way to incomplete thoughts and confusing structures.
* Following along with this, orally practicing allows you to cut down on what does NOT need to be said and focus much more on what IS needed. This requires experience with what resources you have available (i.e. props, powerpoint,etc.) to really drive home your point, alongside some background of your audience.
* The culmination of these is just reviewing the presentation material more, making you more confident as you present.

3. When do you know that a meeting was successful?

* Generally, meetings should have objectives, as in topics that need to be discussed collaboratively. If the meeting accomplished discussing all objectives, this is one major indicator of a successful meeting.
* Another good indicator of a successful meeting is the inclusion of everyone in discussions. If everyone is engaged and was able to speak and be heard, more feel like they contributed to a solution, alongside feeling valued and appreciated for being heard.
