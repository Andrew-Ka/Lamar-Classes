Discussion Board of Lecture 1

1. What is your personal opinion on what is best principle of a good writing?

	Good writing is necessary to be taken seriously. If anyone is to propose an idea, sell a product, or communicate an idea, good writing is necessary.
	Best principles for good writing include clear and concise messages, good grammar, punctuation, and spelling, and must be easily understood by the intended audience. 
	My personal opinion of such goals is that it is necessary to develop good writing in all of these aspects. Clear and concise points clear away any confusion, and good grammar is necessary to not be a distraction.


2. What is your personal opinion on the challenges encountered when we are supposed to deliver an effective communication?

	As we seek to be more professional in our writing, we may turn to the idea that bigger words and longer sentences show a type of professionalism. In actuality, the best and most professional forms of communication are efficient, explaining and clarifying what is necessary and sticking to the basics. This does not exclude "bigger words", but rather the understanding of the context and use of such language is key to usage. If used incorrectly, these techniques will muddy the water more than clarify, leading to less effective communication.


3. Why do you think it is so important to have good communication skills? 

	Good communication is important, as stated before, to be take seriously. Without proper communication, abstract ideas will be turned to jokes, and products would become gags. Even in comedy, good communication is the difference between a well executed joke and a rambling and boring story. 

"Good communication is just as stimulating as black coffee, and just as hard to sleep after." - Anne Morrow Lindbergh
